What Is Submission?

Submission means recognizing God's absolute authority and devoting our worship absolutely to God Alone.

Submission is the religion taught by Abraham, Moses, Jesus, Muhammad, and all of God's messengers.

A Submitter is one who worships God Alone, believes in the Hereafter, and strives to lead a righteous life.

Core Principles

All worship, prayer, devotion, trust, and ultimate authority belong to God Alone.

Quran Alone
