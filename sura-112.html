Sura 112: Absoluteness (Al-Ikhlaas)

In the name of God, Most Gracious, Most Merciful
1. Proclaim, “He is the One and only GOD.
2. “The Absolute GOD.
3. “Never did He beget. Nor was He begotten.
4. “None equals Him.”
