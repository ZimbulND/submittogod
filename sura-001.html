The Key

Chapter 1 • Al-Fātehah • 7 Verses

Introduction

The Key (Al-Fātehah) opens the Quran and serves as the foundation of the Contact Prayers (Salat).

Verses

1:1 In the name of GOD, Most Gracious, Most Merciful.
1:2 Praise be to GOD, Lord of the universe.
1:3 Most Gracious, Most Merciful.
1:4 Master of the Day of Judgment.
1:5 You alone we worship; You alone we ask for help.
1:6 Guide us in the right path;
1:7 the path of those whom You blessed; not of those who have deserved wrath, nor of the strayers.
