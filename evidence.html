The Mathematical Miracle

The Quran contains an extraordinary mathematical structure based on the number 19.

This mathematical miracle serves as a physical, verifiable sign confirming the divine origin and preservation of the Quran.

Quran 74:30

"Over it is nineteen."

Why Number 19?

The number 19 is presented in the Quran as part of a divine sign. According to this understanding, the mathematical structure confirms that the Quran is precisely composed and preserved by God.

This evidence became especially significant in the computer age, when detailed textual patterns could be examined with precision.

Areas of Evidence

Mathematical Structure

Patterns connected to the number 19 appear throughout the Quran's text, chapters, verses, initials, and composition.

Preservation

The mathematical structure serves as a guard against addition, removal, or corruption of the Quranic text.

Verification

The evidence invites readers to investigate, count, compare, and verify rather than accept blindly.

A sincere seeker should ask God for guidance and examine the evidence carefully.
