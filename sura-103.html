Sura 103: The Afternoon (Al-‘Asr)

In the name of God, Most Gracious, Most Merciful
1. By the afternoon.
2. The human being is utterly lost.
3. Except those who believe and lead a righteous life, and exhort one another to uphold the truth, and exhort one another to be steadfast.
