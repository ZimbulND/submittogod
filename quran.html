Read The Quran

The Quran is God's final scripture to humanity and the foundation of Submission.

This website encourages every visitor to read the Quran directly, ask God for guidance, and verify everything personally.

Truth does not fear examination.

Authorized English Version

The Quran translation referenced on this website is the Authorized English Version translated by Rashad Khalifa.

Readers are encouraged to study carefully, reflect deeply, and compare all claims against the Quran itself.

Why Read The Quran Directly?

Direct Guidance

The Quran calls people to worship God Alone and live a righteous life.

Personal Verification

Each person is responsible for seeking, reading, reflecting, and verifying the truth.

Complete Message

The Quran is the foundation for understanding Submission and returning to God Alone.

  • Chapter 2 - The Heifer
  • Chapter 6 - Livestock
  • Chapter 17 - The Children of Israel
  • Chapter 18 - The Cave
  • Chapter 39 - The Throngs
  • Chapter 55 - Most Gracious

Key Reminder

The goal is not merely to read words, but to seek guidance from God, reflect sincerely, and apply the message in daily life.

Ask God to guide you, increase your knowledge, and help you worship Him Alone.
