Sura 106: Quraish (The Quraish Tribe)

In the name of God, Most Gracious, Most Merciful
1. This should be cherished by Quraish.
2. The way they cherish the caravans of the winter and the summer.
3. They shall worship the Lord of this shrine.
4. For He is the One who fed them after hunger, and provided them with security after fear.
