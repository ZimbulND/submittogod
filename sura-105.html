Sura 105: The Elephant (Al-Feel)

In the name of God, Most Gracious, Most Merciful
1. Have you noted what your Lord did to the people of the elephant?
2. Did He not cause their schemes to backfire?
3. He sent upon them swarms of birds.
4. That showered them with hard stones.
5. He made them like chewed up hay.
